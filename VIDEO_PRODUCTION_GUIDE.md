## **Step 4: Video Editing Workflow**

### **In DaVinci Resolve / CapCut:**

1. **Import Media**
   - Video clips
   - Audio files (narration, SFX, music)
   - Images/stills
   - Text overlays

2. **Arrange Timeline**
   - Drag video clips in order
   - Add narration on audio track
   - Add SFX and music

3. **Add Effects**
   - Lightning effects
   - Rain overlays
   - Fog/mist effects
   - Shadow effects
   - Transitions

4. **Color Grading**
   - Scene 1-2: Dark blue (night)
   - Scene 3: Deep blue/purple (bedroom night)
   - Scene 4: Warm golden (morning)
   - Scene 5: Bright white (morning)
   - Scene 6-8: Natural office lighting
   - Scene 9-10: Orange/purple (sunset/dusk)

5. **Add Text Overlays**
   - Scene titles
   - Character names (optional)
   - Episode name and number

6. **Export**
   - Format: MP4 (most compatible)
   - Resolution: 1080p or 4K
   - Frame rate: 24fps or 30fps

## **Step 7: Quality Checklist**

Before exporting, check:

- ✅ All dialogue is clear and audible
- ✅ SFX are properly timed
- ✅ Music doesn't overpower dialogue
- ✅ Color grading is consistent
- ✅ Transitions are smooth
- ✅ No video glitches or artifacts
- ✅ Text is readable
- ✅ Audio levels are balanced
- ✅ Duration is appropriate (16-18 minutes ideal)
- ✅ Video is in 16:9 aspect ratio

## **Tips for Best Results**

1. **Lighting:** Use dark tones for scary scenes, bright for morning scenes
2. **Sound:** Balance is key - dialogue should be heard clearly
3. **Effects:** Less is more - don't overuse effects
4. **Pacing:** Match visuals to audio timing
5. **Consistency:** Keep character representations consistent
6. **Subtitles:** Add Hindi/English subtitles for accessibility
